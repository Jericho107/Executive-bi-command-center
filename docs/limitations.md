# Limitations

- All business units and values are synthetic.
- Thresholds for margin, cash conversion, retention and variance are illustrative.
- The stress test applies controlled shocks and is not a probabilistic risk model.
- Driver rules are transparent heuristics, not causal attribution.
- The HTML output demonstrates a decision surface but is not a production semantic model or deployed executive dashboard.
- No budgeting workflow, write-back, alerting, authentication or production observability is implemented.
- No realised financial impact or intervention result is claimed.
